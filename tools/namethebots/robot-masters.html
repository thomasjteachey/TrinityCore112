How to take part

01 · Submit

Monday to Thursday, every unit in the open wave has its own thread in Discord. Reply with one name. Submit for as many units as you like.

02 · Back a name

React 👍 on the names you want to see win. The ten most-backed per unit go to a poll on Friday. A name two people suggest independently jumps the queue.

03 · Vote

Nine polls, one per unit, open all weekend. Most votes wins outright — no shortlist by committee, no tiebreak by the owner.

04 · It goes live

Sunday night the names are applied to the realm. Queue a battleground and you'll fight them under their new names. Your handle goes in the unit's guild note.

  • 2–12 letters, A–Z only — no numbers, spaces, apostrophes or punctuation.
  • No letter three times in a row — Throgggg is rejected by the client, not by us.
  • Not already taken by a character on the realm.
  • Everything else is fair game. A name is only vetoed if it would get the realm in trouble, and the veto is announced when it happens.
